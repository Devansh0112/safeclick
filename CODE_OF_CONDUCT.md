# Code of Conduct

SafeClick exists to help people, including children and older adults, stay safe. We hold our community to the same care.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps:

- Being kind, patient and welcoming, especially to first-time contributors and people without a technical background
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Never blaming or mocking people who have been scammed, in content or in discussion
- Focusing on what is best for the people who use the site

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks
- Sexualized language or imagery
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Sharing real scam victims' details, or real phone numbers, links or accounts used by scammers, without redaction
- Contributing working malicious code, real phishing kits or instructions that would help someone commit fraud
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a space used by children

## Child safety

Children use this site. Never contact a minor through this project, never ask any contributor or user for personal information, and report any concern to the maintainers immediately.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainers at **CONDUCT-CONTACT@example.org** (replace with a real address before launch). All reports will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone whose behavior they judge inappropriate.
