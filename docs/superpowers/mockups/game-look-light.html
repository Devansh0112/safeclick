Max's Safe Trip: rough mockups

Part 1: pick a look for the level screen. Part 2: one gate, from challenge to caught to level clear. Rough sketches, not final art.

Part 1 · Which look feels right for 8–12 year olds?

Same level in three styles: Max is between Gate 1 (cleared) and Gate 2 (glowing, next challenge). Gate 3 is locked. The flag is point B.

A · Storybook cartoon

Thick outlines, bright warm colours, a bit goofy. Feels like a picture book that moves. Easiest to make friendly villains in this style.

B · Retro pixel art

Chunky blocks, limited colours, arcade feel. Kids read it as "a real video game." Quick to animate, but small details like web addresses are harder to show inside the scene.

C · Soft paper cut-out

Pastel shapes with gentle shadows, like a craft project. Calm and friendly, and it fits SafeClick's current look best. Less "arcade energy" than A or B.

Part 2 · One gate, start to finish (shown in look A)

Habit for this level: read the web address, every letter counts.

1 · The gate

Three doors, three addresses. One is misspelled, one is flashy and "free." Max walks through whichever one the kid taps.

2 · Caught

A goofy villain (not police) nets Max. One line says which red flag was missed. Retry from this gate, not the start.

3 · Level clear

Stars, then the one-line rule to remember, then a "teach a grown-up" mini challenge the kid can show a parent.
